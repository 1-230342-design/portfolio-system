About Artfolio

Our web-based portfolio system helps students store, manage, and showcase their academic works, projects, skills, and achievements in one organized platform — while giving professors an easy way to review, grade, and manage submissions.

Everything you create, in one place.

◧

Organize Your Portfolio

Upload and manage every project in one secure, always-accessible portfolio.

◐

Classwork & Grading

Attach work directly to assignments and get graded on creativity, technique, and composition.

◑

Public Showcase & QR

Make approved works public and share them instantly with a scannable QR code — perfect for exhibits.

◈

Originality Check

Every upload is automatically compared against classmates' work using image and AI tag similarity.
